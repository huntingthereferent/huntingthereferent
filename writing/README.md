[← Profile](../README.md) · [Research](../RESEARCH.md) · [Source and evidence index](../PROVENANCE.md)

**Start with [The Human Wall](THE_HUMAN_WALL.md).** It is the root question of this work: whether the scale of reality and everything we cannot know are carried into smaller problems, creating unnecessary expansion, doubt, or drift that can become normal across layers.

**Attribution.** James supplies observations, compressed wording, and corrections; the assistant proposes interpretations or connections. Each is kept attributable. A useful connection can survive a correction; a mistaken inference does not become fact.

This is collaborative analysis, not a reconstructed private psychology or a substitute for native research evidence.
