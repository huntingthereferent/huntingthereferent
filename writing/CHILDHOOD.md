*James and the assistant · recalled October 2026*

## Reported memories

**Outdoors.** James recalls spending hours in the yard finding lizards, slugs, and Hawaiian blind snakes. He caught and held lizards; now he describes them as delicate and does not particularly want to handle them.

**Diaries.** He collected diaries because he liked reading other people's thoughts. He owned diaries but had difficulty filling them.

**Early internet.** Around middle school, he built a GeoCities page for graffiti tags and artwork from different places.

## Interpretation and limit

I connected these memories to our current discussion of observing, reading, and preserving perspective. James supplied the details; I supplied that connection. None of these childhood activities establishes that he had a later mathematical method in mind.

The useful record is the memory, the offered interpretation, and the correction if the interpretation fails—not a predetermined origin story.
