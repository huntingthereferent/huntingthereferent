# Opinions / Working Positions
*A revisable notebook, not a list of demonstrated laws.*

[← Writing](README.md) · [Profile](../README.md)

These are positions emerging from the investigation and discussions. They are deliberately separated from formal mathematical claims, empirical findings, and medical or psychological conclusions. A counterexample, better explanation, or changed circumstances may revise them.

## Learning and self-correction

**01 — Learning is not the same as earning.**  
Economic return is important, but failure to earn from an activity does not establish that nothing was learned.

**02 — Being able to recognize an error is different from accepting one.**  
The discomfort of self-correction can matter even when nobody else is watching.

**03 — Understanding can already exist before it can be communicated.**  
The absence of an expected explanation does not automatically imply the absence of an underlying relationship.

**04 — Correction can reach the process doing the correcting.**  
A changed answer may change how the next question is framed. This is a description to examine, not a proposed law of cognition.

## AI and making things

**05 — Generative AI changes who can attempt technical work.**  
Access to programming and mathematical tools can precede conventional mastery of their prerequisites.

**06 — Vibe coding requires explicit provenance.**  
The human's observations, prompts, selections, and corrections and the AI's generated implementations should not be conflated with independent technical authorship.

**07 — A generated explanation can be correct and still answer the wrong question.**  
Keeping the intended referent available matters when moving between language, code, models, and tests.

**08 — A working artifact is stronger evidence than a persuasive description, but not evidence of every stronger claim.**  
Tests, real executions, simulations, deployments, and independent repetitions must remain distinguishable.

## Human autonomy

**09 — Assistance should expand participation, not automatically replace judgment.**  
Support needs and decision-making authority are different questions.

**10 — A diagnosis is not a complete description of capacity.**  
Autism does not imply one intelligence profile, communication style, or degree of independence. This position is compatible with substantial, individualized support.

**11 — Other people's opinions are useful input, not compulsory conclusions.**  
Listening and agreeing are separate operations.

**12 — A researcher's vocabulary is not a prerequisite for examining the work.**  
Others can inspect the questions, models, and test results on their own terms.
