# Childhood, Part Two — Reckful and World of Warcraft
*Continuing the conversation about James's earlier gaming years*

## The story does not end at Etopia

The first childhood page followed James through diaries, GeoCities, Quake III, names like Neo and Kong, the people he watched and played against, and the change from remote servers to Counter-Strike games with people in the same room.

Reading it brought something back. James liked seeing the old names included. And when he reached the cheating accusation, he said it felt as though James was finally telling the truth.

That feeling is part of why the writing mattered. He remembered an accusation and how other people responded to it; seeing his own side in a connected story felt different from the old public interpretation. Nothing about the story needs to be turned into a contest over how to label James today.

But James was also quick to point out that we had **not reached the end** of those years.

## Reckful

James named **Reckful** as part of the next section he wanted to recover.

The name is here because James brought it back, not because the assistant knows the rest of Reckful's story. The specific encounters and the relationship between them still need to be carried over from James's earlier account. Filling those in with a generic online-friendship scene would lose the very thing he wanted to keep.

For now, Reckful connects this page to a later stretch of James's gaming memories. The name should not disappear merely because the first telling stops at Quake III and Etopia.

## World of Warcraft

James also said there was **a lot of World of Warcraft** in the story he'd already told.

That matters: WoW is not an incidental game to insert as a one-line transition between Etopia and adult research. It's another part of the online years he wants represented, with stories and people of its own.

The specific characters, events, and moments from those WoW years aren't recovered in the notes available for this edit. We can keep the door open here without writing adventures on James's behalf or reducing them to a tidy explanation of who he would become.

## Keep following the people and the games

The first page showed James repeatedly returning to the movement of other players, to recognition, to misunderstandings, and to people he remembers. The next page is meant to continue the life around those experiences—not convert the games into a theory.

**Reckful and World of Warcraft are the next named parts of that story.** Their detailed scenes belong here when we recover James's words for them.
