## ARC native games — historical claims and published artifacts

The original October 2026 local reports describe:
- **SK48:** 8/8 levels, 280 actions; two reported winning replays.
- **LS20:** 7/7 levels, 309 actions; two reported winning replays.
- **FT09:** 6/6 levels, 75 actions; two reported visual-agent wins, with 81- and 76-action exploratory runs.

Scores of 100 were reported by the local ARC SDK for those specific runs. The public [native ARC research directory](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/blob/main/research/arc_native_runs/README.md) now contains normalized JSON action routes, origin hashes where available, and a [native replay script](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/blob/main/research/arc_native_runs/replay_saved_paths.py). Those are **substantive published inputs**, correcting older documentation that said the routes or replay entrypoint were not public. They are not captured new game scorecards or proof that a fresh native replay was executed.

The [FT09 visible-frame causal agent](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/blob/main/research/arc_native_runs/ft09_visible_causal_agent.py) has also been published, with a remaining external vendor dependency. The SK48 saved route relies on engine-private coordinates, and LS20 is a saved reference-assisted sequence. Full original development context, per-step frame traces, consistent SDK/environment versions, and independent replays remain incomplete. [Detailed source distinctions](ARC_EVIDENCE.md).

**Necessary negative control:** [autonomous public-frame trial records](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/blob/main/research/arc_native_runs/AUTONOMOUS_TRIALS.md) report SK48, LS20, and AR25 *unsolved* under those separate trials. Those results cannot be rewritten as the winning outcomes of the saved-route experiments.

The [native frame observer](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/blob/main/runtime/arc_native_observer.py) and [observer tests](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/blob/main/network/tests/test_arc_native_observer.py) inspect executed transitions; the observer does not decide or execute the game's actions. An optional real-SDK single-step case is distinct from a whole-game replay. ARC-like [finite mathematical research](ARC_EVIDENCE.md#03--arc-like-finite-field-mathematical-research--public-solvers-and-tests) is not an ARC-AGI-3 native-game solve.

## Finite mathematics — published continuation is no longer only local

The older local case reported an 81-state B-invariant fiber. A negative pair exhibits nine 9-cycles versus three 27-cycles and diverging fixed-point counts at `B^9`. A separate positive case exhibits an explicit restricted B-intertwining bijection. Earlier locally reported runs included 13/13 continuation and 17/17 re-entry checks.

Since those initial reports, [Cyclic Action Engine](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/blob/main/work/arc_like_maze/CYCLIC_ACTION_ENGINE.md) and [its tests](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/blob/main/network/tests/test_arc_like_cyclic_action_engine.py), plus [ConstructedEngine continuation](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/blob/main/work/arc_like_maze/CYCLIC_ACTION_CONTINUATION.md) and [its tests](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/blob/main/network/tests/test_arc_like_cyclic_action_continuation.py), have been published. The contracts describe exact finite domains, source laws, recorded observations, certificates, snapshots, and replay/entry rules. Thus the blanket historical statement that *no exact laws, source code, positive-bijection implementation or runnable tests are published* is **outdated**.

These are bounded mathematical claims with published reproduction entrypoints, **not** an independent run performed as part of this provenance edit. An 81-state B-invariant fiber need not be A-invariant: B-only conjugacy never silently becomes full two-generator conjugacy. An inconclusive budget remains UNKNOWN; an earlier distinguishing observation cannot be erased by a later coincident count. The broader A/B referent remains language for relational movement, not an equation or one finite system.
