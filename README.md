# Research system — current state and expansion capacity

**Independent research · finite mathematics · language · computational experiments · research infrastructure**

## System status

The public repositories document approximately one month of development during September–October 2026, alongside earlier investigative groundwork. The current implementation spans mathematical constructions, language and referent research, ARC and computational-world experiments, scientific data comparison, source-preserving handoffs, worker processes, and audit records.

The workstation is a general-purpose research and construction environment. GitHub currently supplies source control and portions of the execution and evidence infrastructure; it is not the intended capacity boundary or the complete product.

## Expansion model

The working pattern is **construct → test → audit → retain evidence → continue**. A supported output can be considered for a further pass, and additional workers or branches can be introduced under explicit evidence, authorization, and resource conditions. Reaching a cap calls for inspecting an admissible alternate construction or expanding available resources; it does not guarantee unlimited continuation.

| Existing research / infrastructure | Capacity unlocked by expansion | Potential new capability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finite A/B mathematics and construction engines | More CPU cores, RAM, parallel search, formal verification tools | Larger candidate spaces, deeper correspondence checks, independent proof and counterexample searches |

### Evidence and verification

The standard is source-first: identify the native referent, record an intervention and its returned result, compare against a prediction, and retain counterexamples. A passing test verifies its stated boundary; it does not by itself demonstrate a general solution, a physical measurement, or a successful external deployment.

**Selected source-pinned evidence — reviewed October 21, 2026** ([frozen source revision](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/commit/7916709e16e14cb1222639f385746abc882effe1)): the original ARC-AGI-3 **SP80 SDK reached level 4 of 6** in two independently reproduced native entries ([original level-4 witness](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/actions/runs/39616395169)); a later [52-reset original-SDK level-4 experiment](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/actions/runs/39636174534) returned **0 level-5/WIN advances**. Two authenticated AACT exports contain **1,345 differing study-status fields among 432,597 shared IDs** ([source comparison](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/blob/7916709e16e14cb1222639f385746abc882effe1/systems/workspace/NIH_P2_STATUS_DIFFERENCE_SEARCH.md)); their depositors report November **4** and **9, 2022** collection dates, respectively ([owner-attributed date evidence](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/blob/7916709e16e14cb1222639f385746abc882effe1/systems/workspace/NIH_P2_AACT_OWNER_DATE_LINK.md)), but the original per-study version-publication chronology remains unverified. A/B WORLD [Trial 31](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/actions/runs/39531157339) found that **3 of 3 hypothetical fourth-response cases** exceeded the sufficiency of at least one conservative clearance bound before 0.25 virtual-time units, with **5 no-response controls**; this is **not** a measured fifth impact or a physical collision proof. These remain separate, bounded results, not one combined proof.

### Research method

A bounded test records its source, predicted change, permitted action or transformation, returned result, and independent comparison. Failed predictions remain visible. Mathematical correspondence, executable behavior, language interpretation, and physical measurement each require their own evidence.
