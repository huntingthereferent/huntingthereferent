Language is already changing. Words and meanings shift over time as people, contexts, and relationships change. I use **Language Referent** (earlier **A/B**) to follow what is actually being referred to through those changes, rather than assume an earlier description still holds.

I feel continuity as **toroidal movement**: movement, relationships, and the changing space belong together, rather than movement taking place inside a fixed container. Finite wraparound models give me a **mathematical witness** for tracking changes while checking that a relationship's identity and provenance remain recoverable. The **recursion test** asks whether the output of one cycle can become valid input for the next under the same evidence contract, without silently replacing the relationship or losing unresolved differences. The mathematics helps test and record continuity; it is not the referent itself.

**Tracework** carries these checks into work with AI and computational experiments, keeping sources, observations, and corrections available as the work continues.
