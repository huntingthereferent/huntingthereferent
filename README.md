Hi, I'm James. I like understanding how things work. I follow questions through language, mathematics, software, and games, often learning the most when someone else's perspective changes what I thought I was seeing.

I use generative AI to help turn observations into code and experiments. I direct the questions, check the results, and keep the corrections. This is a place to share what I can actually show, including the parts that aren't finished.

## Writing

A lot of what I investigate starts with other people: reading what they wrote, playing alongside them, or working through a conversation. When we misunderstand each other, the correction is part of the record too. It's how I get another point of view instead of only my own.
