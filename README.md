Language is already in motion. Words and meanings change over time as people use them in different contexts. I follow the relationships through those changes, returning to what was actually referred to rather than assuming the same words still mean the same thing. **Language Referent** (earlier **A/B**) names that continuing relationship, not the words used to describe it.

I describe this flow as a **moving torus**: movement, changing relationships, and the changing container belong together. Toroidal movement gives me a way to trace a relationship through change without losing track of where it came from. Finite mathematical models let me record each step and check whether its result can become the next input. The mathematics is a **witness**, not the referent itself. **Tracework** keeps sources, observations, and corrections available as the work continues.
