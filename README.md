**Independent researcher · finite mathematics · experimental systems · source-based verification**

I develop mathematical constructions and examine them in executable systems. The work asks what changes under defined operations, what remains invariant, and where a proposed relationship fails. The underlying research predates this GitHub account and its public viewers.

**Tracework** is the working environment developed alongside that research. It carries distinct investigations through source-pinned implementations, handoffs, tests, observations, and recorded returns. It retains the connection between a problem and the evidence produced as work continues. Managing AI drift is one practical use of this continuity; a passing workflow is not, by itself, evidence of a mathematical result.

## Method and scope

Specify the system and admissible actions, predict a return, execute at the relevant native boundary, compare the actual return, and preserve both witnesses and counterexamples. Keep **proposed**, **simulated**, **observed**, **independently checked**, and **still unresolved** distinct. Tracework can retain that history while work moves, but it does not replace the native source or proof criteria.

The work is independent and does not claim institutional affiliation or a completed universal theory. The current platform includes opt-in and manually admitted operations; a public viewer is not proof of a fully unattended hosted system.

**Reported direct cost:** $20 for the presented development setup. **Time:** documented work spans September–October 2026, with earlier mathematical groundwork predating this record; total working hours have not been measured. Monetary cost and time spent are different measures.

Everything presented here, **including the wording on this page**, has been developed and revised through Tracework (the environment previously called the AI-workstation) as part of the live research process. Generated language is not itself evidence; the source definitions, returned observations, tests and documented limits remain authoritative.
