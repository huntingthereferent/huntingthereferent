### The investigation

> **A person can spend years learning and still come to believe they are failing.**

The starting point was not a mathematics project. Years of learning and making connections across different subjects had not reliably produced an income. The difference between intellectual movement and economic results affected confidence in abilities already being used.

The original use of generative AI was an attempt to improve communication. **The referent was pointed at James while he was solving problems.** A/B arose as language from observations of that activity—not as a preselected mathematical theory or an explanation applied afterward.

Recognizing an existing way of understanding improved confidence. An unexpected second change followed: AI began helping translate grounded questions into technical constructions more efficiently. Earlier coding ideas were revisited, and AI suggested mathematical directions that the investigation went on to explore.

The result is a growing body of **AI-assisted, vibe-coded software, formal experiments, test harnesses, and research documentation**, with the technical evidence kept separate from the personal history.

## Evidence and authorship

<details>
<summary><strong>How this work was produced</strong></summary>

All software, mathematical formalization, code, tests and research documentation in this project history were developed through **generative-AI-assisted vibe coding**.

The originating human contribution includes the observed problem-solving behavior, the effort to make the referent communicable, project direction, interpretation, selection and correction. The original A/B label comes from looking at James solving problems; later formal `A` and `B` transformations are scoped mathematical objects, not an explanation of his mind.

Code in a repository establishes availability. Passing tests, local observations, simulation, independent reproduction, deployment, and paid adoption remain separate evidence claims.
