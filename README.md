I investigate **continuity** through the **Language Referent**: what remains referred to as language and relationships change. I describe that movement geometrically as a **moving torus**. **A/B** names the relational movement I follow; it is not conventional A/B testing.

I use finite mathematical and computational harnesses to examine particular relationships. **Tracework** grew from using AI across conversations and experiments while keeping provenance, corrections, and source records accessible. It is still early-stage work. Existing artifacts do not establish how much came from the mathematics, AI, or their interaction, and they do not yet validate the broader geometric account.
