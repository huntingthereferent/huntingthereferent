## The ARC correction, including the failure

Earlier records described a real original SP80 SDK result at **2/6**, then independently verified negative level-2 geometry tests. Those records remain legitimate for their exact tested programs. They no longer describe the highest observed state. The [frozen finite-screen contrast](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/blob/702326a0d9c0dc8e9225b4cdd4d9a8c333b778f5/work/arc3/native_sp80_stage2_finite_flow_contrast.py) identified a level-3 action word by evaluating proposals **in the real SDK**, not by assuming the proposed screen-flow model was the engine. Two genuine native histories reached level 3; the [follow-up reproduction](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/blob/702326a0d9c0dc8e9225b4cdd4d9a8c333b778f5/work/arc3/native_sp80_stage3_entry_source_map.py) verified the same entered screen.

The separate [stage-3 native feedback run](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/actions/runs/39471887855) reported `actual_original_source_level4_or_WIN: 0`, with **32/32 remaining level 3, NOT_FINISHED**. This is precisely the kind of negative that belongs beside the positive witness. It does not certify the stage-3 objective or exclude other action sequences.

## What a research result has to carry

For each investigation, retain the source revision, admitted input, native permitted action or defined operator, returned state or original record, independent observer or verifier (if one exists), counterexample, and the next unresolved boundary.

- **Native game outcome:** the original game's `levels_completed` or `WIN`, never a proxy animation or plausible geometry.
- **Mathematical witness:** an exact declared model, verified computation, assumptions and counterexamples. It does not transfer by analogy.
- **Source-data discrepancy:** original provenance, field meaning, publication-versus-extraction chronology, identifiers and missing versions.
- **Workflow pass:** an outcome of a particular test job at its recorded commit SHA, not a blanket claim about all deployments or all real-world cases.
- **Worker completion:** an admitted actual job and its original journal/owner verification, not the existence of a worker implementation.

Unverified branches and proposals are not silently included as finished work on the source snapshot. Source-owned work can continue; follow a new revision before promoting any newer claim.
